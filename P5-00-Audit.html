Design audit and corrections

Every screen scored 1–5 on 20 criteria before and after correction. Defects were found by reading every route's data and template output, and by automated checks run during the build: link lens, trust-count consistency, chart questions, KPI limits and markup balance. All critical and high-priority defects are corrected; residuals are listed. No decorative complexity was added.
