# Hardcoded values register

This is the one place that lists what the prototype shows but **cannot derive from plant data**. Everything else is calculated bottom-up (Plant → Entity → Group) by `data/plant-model/` and written into `js/data/base-data.js` by `export_to_prototype.py`.

> This file is regenerated by `python3 data/plant-model/export_to_prototype.py`. Edit the narrative part in `data/plant-model/hardcoded_header.md`, not here.

## What is derived (not in this register)

- **Plant KPIs:** the 31 plant KPIs in `data/Plant-KPIs.csv`, plus aliases SIG-001/002/003, OPS-005 and OPS-006. Scopes covered are Plant01–Plant06, A1, A2 and Group for period P06 (Sep 2026), with the P01–P06 history behind them. See `data/plant-model/Data-Model-Methodology.md`.
- **Model-bound tables and bar charts:** any table with `kcols` and any bar chart with `kpi`. Examples are G-05's plant benchmark, G-02's entity matrix (production column), O-09's production by entity, and E-01's plant bars.

## Hierarchy

- **In use:** Group → Entity A1 (Plants 01–03), Entity A2 (Plants 04–06). "Business A" in breadcrumbs is the Group.
- **Removed:** Entities B1, B2, C1, C2 and Businesses B and C.
- **Reassigned:** items that belonged to removed entities moved to A1 or A2. These are capex projects, the MP03 contractor slippage alert, the TRU-011 variance note, and the routing drafts.
