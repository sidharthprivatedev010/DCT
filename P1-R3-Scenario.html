Glossary (controlled vocabulary)

Term
Definition in this product
Lens
A depth of view (Owner, Core Group, Entity). It never grants scope.
Scope
The hierarchy nodes and domains a user is entitled to see.
Business status
How a measure performs: On track, Improving, Deteriorating, Breached, Forecast breach.
Trust status
Whether a number is defensible: Certified, Certified with exception, Pending certification, Reconciliation break, Stale, Missing, Restricted.
Provenance
Class of a value: certified actual, preliminary actual, forecast, scenario, external signal, AI-generated.
Leading signal
An early indicator that may predict an outcome; not itself an outcome.
Prediction output
A modelled forward value (days to breach, probability, projected gap). Always shown in forecast style.
Alert
A system-detected, validated deviation or forecast breach.
Case
An alert with an accepted human owner, holding actions and evidence.
Incident
A case at materiality ≥ High with an Incident Commander.
War room
Collaboration mode of an incident.
Materiality
Placeholder-scored significance (Critical, High, Medium, Low) with visible drivers.
Owner threshold
The materiality level at which an issue is pushed to the Owner [PH].
Accountability strip
Severity, exposure, owner role, due date, escalation clock, evidence state, next action.
Escalation clock
Rule-based time remaining before automatic escalation to the next level.
Disclosure clock
Time and state of disclosure review, where applicable [PH]; no legal rule is modelled.
Certification
A human Certifier's decision that a KPI value for a period is defensible.
Certified with exception
Certified, with a recorded limitation and a due date for its resolution.
Reconciliation break
A variance between two reconciled points that exceeds tolerance [PH].
Lineage
The path from source placeholder to KPI to leadership views.
Evidence
An immutable file, link or attestation supporting a fact, action or closure.
Closure
Human-approved end of a case once its criteria are met; reopenable under control.
Decision
A recorded human choice with authority role, rationale and options considered.
Scenario
A labelled what-if with explicit assumptions; never the base forecast.
